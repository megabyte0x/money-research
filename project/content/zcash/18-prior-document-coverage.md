# 18 — Coverage of the Earlier Research

## Scope and method

This map covers all **44 original Markdown documents**: 13 in Gold, 14 in After Gold, and 17 in Bitcoin. Every file was included in the chapter inventory and thematic comparison. Monetary-regime, credit, legal-tender, and concluding chapters received additional close reading. This is a synthesis of their questions and lessons, not a fresh line-by-line factual certification of all 71,066 words in the original collection.

The table identifies how each earlier document informed the new research. A connection is not an endorsement of every claim in that document. Chapter 01 explicitly revises several overly strong conclusions; current technical and legal claims have their own [source register](17-sources-and-evidence-register.md).

The Bitcoin directory uses actual numeric filenames such as `07.md`. Links below follow those files rather than the descriptive filenames listed in its original reading guide.

## What this volume adds

1. A current Zcash-specific technical account, including 2026 security remediation, Ironwood, migration, and proposal status.
2. A mechanism-by-mechanism comparison of what private settlement can solve and what it cannot.
3. An operational definition of a ZEC unit of account, separated from fiat-priced cryptocurrency payments.
4. A politically willing hypothetical society, concrete adoption stages, balance sheets, and failure scenarios.
5. Measurements that could support or reject the hypothesis instead of assuming adoption follows from technical merit.
