# Package 09 comparison evidence matrix

Reviewed 15 September 2026. This is a **publication gate matrix**, not a scored verdict. A cell is ready only when a claim in the shared registry is `accepted` with an exact source locator, its stated perspective/use/arrangement is covered, contrary evidence and uncertainty are recorded, and the coordinator passes the claim through the generated model. Since the initial matrix, the coordinator accepted five narrowly scoped cases: Monzo account transfer processing, Bitcoin main-chain authorization/confirmation, Bank of England note household offer and merchant acceptance choice, and a partial Circle LLC USDC Type B issuer-contract right. The other arrangement/use/perspective cells remain **review**, even where an editor corrected chapter wording. These five cases cannot rank arrangements or establish cross-border settlement, long-term purchasing power or pricing suitability.

Arrangement definitions for the proposed comparison: physical gold means an identified bar/coin held by its owner or a specified vault; fiat cash means a specified domestic note and issuing jurisdiction; bank deposit means a named bank, account class and deposit-protection jurisdiction; self-custodied BTC means the key holder controls a Bitcoin main-chain output; custodial BTC means a named exchange or custody agreement; the stablecoin candidate is **Circle Internet Financial LLC's USDC on Ethereum for a US holder**, with wallet versus Circle Mint account and redemption eligibility recorded separately. Its EEA issuer and rights must not be merged with this US case. These definitions are candidate scopes, not public factual cells.

Each requested claim packet must state the original E-ID or `09-` proposal ID; assertion type; household, merchant or central-bank perspective; arrangement, use, jurisdiction, provider/account/network; old/new assertion; source publisher/title/URL/type/publication and access dates/exact locator; contrary evidence; review state; and affected home, chapter, glossary, timeline, comparison and chart locations. Relevant dimensions are supply policy, issuer liability, custody, price stability, settlement assurance, recourse, privacy, censorship exposure, infrastructure, liquidity and credit provision. A qualitative claim still needs a locator. No TPS, volume, return or reserve-share ranking is admissible without comparable denominators and methods.

BIS Basel CRE20.110(1)(b), NSF30.31(4), and LCR30.40–.47 distinguish conditional gold credit-risk treatment, funding and liquid-asset rules. National adoption, account class, insurer rules and bank-payment settlement source are still required. Keep reserves, deposits, Treasury debt and QE distinct.
- **E26 review:** Package 03 corrected the generic stablecoin definition from BIS, *The future monetary system* (2022), ch. III, paragraphs beginning “The two main types of stablecoin...”. This cannot establish USDC terms or reserve sufficiency. Circle's [USDC Terms](https://www.circle.com/legal/usdc-terms), §§2, 4 and “Claim on funds”, distinguish Circle Mint users and other holders, conditional redemption and lack of deposit insurance for Mint-held USDC. Pin the version date, issuer entity, US applicability, Ethereum token contract, dated independent reserve report, reserve custodians and contrary/legal evidence before accepting a cell. Circle's [transparency page](https://www.circle.com/transparency) is an issuer source, not itself an independent audit.
- **E15/E25/E28 review:** Package 04 uses Bitcoin Core `gettxoutsetinfo`, the Bitcoin developer guide's block-chain explanation and Weber (2016) §§2–4 to distinguish issuance from spendable supply and base money from credit. Validation, miner inclusion, confirmations, custodian powers, key compromise, access and coercion are separate. No absolute finality, censorship immunity or dated security ranking is accepted.
- **E11 review:** Package 04's Nigeria CEX purchase share and IMF estimated inflow share have different populations. Neither is a global payment measure or a cross-border settlement outcome.
- **E21/E22 withheld numerical history:** Package 02 removed a mixed gold/silver series and continuous-denarius fineness claim. They cannot support present physical-gold performance or rank metals against BTC.
- **06 chart disposition:** All eleven former numeric candidates remain withheld. No withdrawn bar, chart point or inferred causal caption enters this matrix.
