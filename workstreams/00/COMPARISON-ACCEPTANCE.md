Package 09 proposed two qualitative household-payment packets in [its evidence matrix](../09/EVIDENCE-MATRIX.md). The coordinator opened each publisher page and checked the exact locators before accepting `C09-monzo-fps-payment` and `C09-btc-mainchain-payment` into the shared source/claim model. Their matching cells alone appear for **Everyday payment · Household**; other arrangements, uses and perspectives remain visibly pending. These process claims do not rank payment speed, cost, safety or economic suitability.

The Monzo case names **Monzo Bank Limited's UK personal Current Account** under [terms version 2.11 dated 18 August 2026](https://monzo.com/legal/terms-and-conditions). The terms' “Making payments” and “Bank transfer” sections specify available balance, recipient details, app approval, refusal/delay conditions and a possible undo window. [Pay.UK's Faster Payments explanation](https://www.wearepay.uk/what-we-do/payment-systems/faster-payment-system/how-faster-payments-work/) describes recipient-funds availability and mistaken-payment recovery limits. Neither page certifies the account's interbank final-settlement timing, universal bank-deposit behavior, deposit-insurance eligibility or a card payment via Faster Payments.

The Bitcoin case uses the [developer guide on wallets](https://developer.bitcoin.org/devguide/wallets.html), [transaction construction](https://developer.bitcoin.org/devguide/transactions.html) and [payment verification](https://developer.bitcoin.org/devguide/payment_processing.html). Key control, signed UTXO spending, broadcast, block inclusion and later confirmations are distinct steps. The payment guide explicitly says broadcast alone does not assure payment and treats confirmations as increasing confidence; [Bitcoin Core 29.0.0 `gettransaction`](https://bitcoincore.org/en/doc/29.0.0/rpc/wallet/gettransaction/) independently exposes confirmation, conflict and block fields. The guide has older numerical anecdotes, so no timing, attack-cost, confirmation-count or prevalence assertion was accepted from them. Cash-out, legal recourse, merchant refund, fee and price behavior remain separate.

The accepted sources and claims are in `public/content/sources.json` and `claims.json`; the two cited cell records are in `comparison-cells.json`. `src/comparison-cells.js` requires an accepted comparison claim and exact source ID, URL, title and locator matches before a cell reaches the generated index. The existing component performs its own publication gate. This is a partial V01 evidence step, not a complete six-arrangement comparison or release sign-off.
