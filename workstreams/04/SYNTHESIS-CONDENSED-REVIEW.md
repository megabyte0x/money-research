## Paragraph 6 — ACCEPT, Bitcoin conceptual copy only

Current (verbatim):

> Bitcoin changes base-asset and validation rules. A validating node can check blocks and permitted subsidy under its chosen rules without one issuer's ledger. Investment and some transfers are observed; broad native Bitcoin wages, prices and debts in a large economy are not established here. The condensed text preserves the earlier review's distinction between permitted subsidy and actual/spendable balances, independent node validation and a conditional credit/velocity model. “Investment and some transfers” is an appropriately limited conceptual description, **not** acceptance of a current volume, market share or nationwide denomination measurement.
