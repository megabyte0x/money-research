# Measurement definitions

Account-dependent Search Console and Bing ownership remain pending until those accounts are available. Do not invent traffic or ranking baselines.

## Indexed canonical coverage

Count of sitemap URLs that remain 200 with matching canonicals, versus redirects, 404s and `noindex` utilities.

## Non-brand queries

Queries that do not contain “Money Research”. Record query, country/language, date, intent and owning URL when Search Console is connected.

## Organic landing engagement

Landings on canonical HTML that continue to a chapter, open a source, or open a volume hub. Do not collect search box text or selected passages beyond what is needed for those events.

## Identifiable AI referral traffic

Referrers that name an AI product. A mention without a link is not a citation. Missing referral data is not proof of no AI visibility. Google AI-feature traffic stays inside Search Console Web reporting.
