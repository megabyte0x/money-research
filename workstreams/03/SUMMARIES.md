## Cross-view definitions for package 08

- **Money creation:** commercial-bank lending creates a customer deposit and loan claim; a central bank's asset purchase creates reserve balances and, for a non-bank seller, a matching bank deposit; government borrowing issues a liability to fund spending. These balance sheets and constraints differ. Bank of England, *Money creation in the modern economy*, Figures 1–3 and pp. 16–20, 24–25.
- **Stablecoin:** a token targeting a value, with asset-backed and algorithmic models; a named issuer's reserves, legal entitlement, redemption eligibility and liquidity must be inspected. BIS, *The future monetary system*, chapter III, stablecoin types.
- **CBDC:** a digital central-bank liability whose access, privacy, remuneration and limits depend on the design; the digital euro is not yet issued. ECB project progress and design FAQs, checked 15 September 2026.
- **Sanctions and payments:** financial restrictions can affect bank messaging, correspondent access or asset claims; these are separate layers, and immobilisation is different from confiscation. Swift, *What is Swift?*; Council of the EU, October 2024 ERA mechanism.
