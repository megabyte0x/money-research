## Paragraph 3 — 1971 and Triffin

Current, verbatim:

> In August 1971 the United States suspended foreign officials' Bretton Woods dollar–gold conversion promise. Paper money and bank credit already existed. Exchange-rate changes followed in stages; the IMF's later amendment changed international gold rules separately. Oil shocks overlapped with inflation already under way, so inflation cannot be assigned to the suspension alone. Triffin described a conditional Bretton Woods tension: more foreign **official** dollar claims held as reserves could supply liquidity but strain conversion confidence if no alternative reserve source grew. This was no universal trade-deficit or inevitable-run law. Read [why the dollar displaced gold](/gold/08-why-the-dollar-replaced-gold/) and [the break after 1971](/after/01-the-break-1971-1976/).

Required replacement, verbatim:

> In August 1971 the United States suspended foreign monetary authorities' conversion of official dollar holdings into gold under Bretton Woods. Paper money and bank credit already existed. Exchange-rate changes followed in stages; the IMF's later amendment changed international gold rules separately. Oil shocks overlapped with inflation already under way, so inflation cannot be assigned to the suspension alone. Triffin described a conditional Bretton Woods tension: foreign official dollar claims held as reserves could supply liquidity, yet could grow relative to US gold available for official conversion and strain confidence in that promise. This was no universal trade-deficit or inevitable-run law. Read [why the dollar displaced gold](/gold/08-why-the-dollar-replaced-gold/) and [the break after 1971](/after/01-the-break-1971-1976/).

The current “foreign officials' ... promise” compressed away the holder/claim distinction from the earlier `SYNTHESIS-REVIEW.md`. Its “if no alternative reserve source grew” condition also ceased to name the **dollar-claims/US-gold conversion** relationship. After Gold 01/02 summary approval supports separate suspension, exchange and IMF changes, plus pre-existing inflation. This accepts the historical framing editorially, **not** a measured claims-to-gold stock ratio or one-cause inflation assessment. Gold editor's official-reserve locators and package 01 must still support any dated/legal statement before publication.

## Paragraph 5 — gold roles and denominators

Current, verbatim:

> When major currencies ceased fixed gold redemption, monetary authorities and households could still hold gold as an asset. A reserve holding or basket does not define wages, taxes and debts by a gold weight. At market value, a higher gold price can raise gold's reserve share without purchases or restored redemption. Gold's share of **broad official reserves**, which include gold, and a currency's share of **foreign-exchange reserves**, which exclude it, have different denominators. Trade invoicing, reserves, debt denomination and payments are separate measures; movement in one does not determine the others. This copy quotes no live figure. Any number needs an accepted observation with period, valuation convention and source. Read [gold's present role](/gold/09-gold-today-what-still-holds-its-value/) and [recent monetary shocks](/after/09-pandemic-inflation-and-weaponized-reserves-2020-2026/).
