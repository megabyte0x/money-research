## Paragraph 1 — current verbatim

> Money can serve several jobs. A unit of account states prices, wages or debts in a specified arrangement. A means of payment transfers value; final settlement depends on the arrangement. An asset may be held for later use, though its purchasing power can change. These jobs can belong to one instrument or separate. Holding gold or Bitcoin does not show that wages are priced in it; accepting a Bitcoin payment does not establish native tax, credit or wage denomination. A rising price over one period alone cannot establish reliable purchasing-power protection over another period or a general inflation/crisis hedge. Gold served ornament and weighed-metal roles and, in some regions and periods, coin and standard roles. The Bitcoin chapters test these roles separately for its protocol and surrounding institutions. Read [metal to money](/gold/03-from-metal-to-money-weights-rings-coins/) and [Bitcoin's demonstrated uses](/bitcoin/02-what-bitcoin-solved-and-what-it-did-not/).

**Decision:** **WITHHOLD full-paragraph approval from After Gold.** Gold/Bitcoin own weighed-metal history, Bitcoin uses and the period-matched real purchasing-power sentence. This paragraph correctly avoids a universal hedge verdict, but the gate has no matched real-return dataset and linked chapters do not certify one.

## Paragraph 2 — current verbatim

> Gold's monetary role depended on institutions as well as physical properties. Gold could be weighed and assayed, but those properties alone did not specify issuer liabilities. Issuing authorities set coin standards under particular laws; markets and users affected acceptance. Silver and copper often handled smaller transactions. Notes and bank deposits were distinct claims. In a specified gold-convertible arrangement, a note could promise redemption at a defined parity while banks issued claims against fractional reserves. If redeemable claims grew relative to reserves, meeting conversion requests could become difficult. Bimetallism and gold standards involved different rules and strains, not one universal metal-backed ladder. Read [other metals and fixed ratios](/gold/05-silver-copper-bronze-and-bimetallism/) and [the gold-standard era](/gold/07-the-gold-standard-era-1717-1971/).

**Decision:** **WITHHOLD full-paragraph approval from After Gold.** Gold owns coin standards, acceptance, bimetallism and redemption history. The current physical-metal/issuer distinction is conceptually narrow; country and era rules and any reserve coverage remain outside this editor's source review.

## Paragraph 3 — current verbatim

> In August 1971 the United States suspended its Bretton Woods promise to convert foreign monetary authorities' dollar holdings into gold. Paper money and bank credit already existed. Exchange-rate changes followed in stages; the IMF's later amendment changed international gold rules separately. Oil shocks overlapped with inflation already under way, so inflation cannot be assigned to the suspension alone. Triffin described a conditional Bretton Woods tension: if additional international reserves relied on foreign official dollar claims, those claims could supply liquidity while growing relative to US gold available for official conversion and straining confidence in that promise. This was no universal trade-deficit or inevitable-run law. Read [why the dollar displaced gold](/gold/08-why-the-dollar-replaced-gold/) and [the break after 1971](/after/01-the-break-1971-1976/).

**Decision:** **QUALIFY.** The current combined Triffin mechanism is within the corrected After Gold 01/02 framing, but the opening should identify the **US official conversion obligation** and foreign monetary authorities as holders rather than compressing the two into a vague promise. Use Replacement 3 below. IMF history ch. 25 and Federal Reserve History support the 1971/1973 sequence in the gate; IMF 2001 ch. III p. 91 and the 1964 US task-force pp. 44–47 support a conditional official-reserve tension. No measured claims/gold ratio, compulsory trade deficit, inevitable run or one-cause inflation inference is accepted.

## Paragraph 4 — current verbatim

> The post-gold system relies on institutions with different jobs. Governments set legal obligations; central banks influence reserves and liquidity; commercial-bank lending creates deposits subject to capital, funding and borrower constraints. Payment networks transmit instructions, while settlement systems transfer balances. Debt crises and cross-border funding pressures have tested these arrangements. The chapters follow Latin American and emerging-market crises, the 2007–09 crash, then asset purchases and emergency responses. They do not establish that each rule failed or any response delivered a measured benefit. Each response's size and effects need a defined period, measure and source. Read [managed-dollar crises](/after/03-debt-crises-and-the-managed-dollar-1982-1990/) and [the financial crisis and QE](/after/07-financial-crisis-and-the-age-of-qe-2007-2019/).

**Decision:** **ACCEPT exact conceptual copy within After Gold scope.** Commercial-bank lending creates deposits with borrower, capital and funding constraints (Bank of England 2014, pp. 16–20). Payment instructions and settlement balances are distinct; C09 Monzo/Pay.UK illustrates one UK customer process, not universal interbank finality. The paragraph explicitly withholds a measured response benefit. Linked debt/crash chronology, rescue amounts, distributions and each rule's national effect still need claim-level sources.

## Paragraph 5 — current verbatim

> After the United States suspended official dollar-to-gold conversion and the IMF later ended the gold-based par-value rule, monetary authorities and households could still hold gold as an asset. A reserve holding or basket does not define wages, taxes and debts by a gold weight. At market value, a higher gold price can raise gold's reserve share without purchases or restored redemption. Gold's share of **broad official reserves**, which include gold, and a currency's share of **foreign-exchange reserves**, which exclude it, have different denominators. Trade invoicing, reserves, debt denomination and payments are separate measures; movement in one does not determine the others. This copy quotes no live figure. Any number needs a period, denominator, valuation convention and source. Read [gold's present role](/gold/09-gold-today-what-still-holds-its-value/) and [recent monetary shocks](/after/09-pandemic-inflation-and-weaponized-reserves-2020-2026/).

**Decision:** **ACCEPT exact conceptual copy within After Gold scope.** The US official conversion suspension and later IMF par-value change are distinct, and gold holdings were not extinguished. ECB E04 Chart 7(a) and IMF COFER Q1 2026 use broad-official-reserves versus foreign-exchange-only denominators. This paragraph quotes no live figure. Any later number needs date, denominator, valuation, revision and locator; a price-driven share gain is not proof of purchases or motive. Gold 02 must approve its cross-volume wording.

## Paragraph 7 — current verbatim

> Bitcoin control depends on the action. Nodes validate against chosen rules; miners and pools construct blocks and affect inclusion; developers propose code; custodians can restrict client access; keys can be compromised or coerced; and authorities regulate within their reach. No single actor has the same power over validation, inclusion, key control and lawful access in every arrangement. Beneficial owners, ETF trusts, shareholders and custodians can describe overlapping interests in the same coins; mining templates differ from node validation. Country “adoption” likewise needs a named policy or legal act and responsible entity. Payment acceptance, seized or finally forfeited assets, state-linked mining, public investment and central-bank reserves are distinct statuses. They cannot be added into an unverified holding or treated as necessary steps toward a Bitcoin standard. Read [supply and control](/bitcoin/09-supply-and-control-who-holds-bitcoin-and-who-benefits/) and [country experiments](/bitcoin/04-countries-that-adopted-bitcoin-and-what-happened/).

**Decision:** **WITHHOLD full-paragraph approval from After Gold.** Bitcoin 04/evidence 01 own node/miner/pool power, overlapping beneficial interests, country legal statuses and seized-versus-forfeited holdings. No pool-share, country total or adoption figure is asserted; each concrete example still needs named law, entity, date and locator.

A stablecoin targets another asset's value; the label alone establishes neither cash/Treasury backing, every holder's redemption right, deposit insurance nor solvency. Historical crisis comparisons identify possible run and contagion channels, not identical national crises or a forecast of their scale. Replacement 8 states only a designed target value and retains token-specific reserves, holder rights, insurance and solvency limits. Corrected After Gold 12 and BIS *The future monetary system*, chapter III, support asset-backed and algorithmic variants. Bitcoin 04/evidence 01 own run, proof-of-reserves and crisis analogies.
