## Date model, overlap and reader needs

`eventDateRange` stores sortable first/last bounds, precision and approximation metadata while leaving historical display dates visible. Signed historical years omit year zero (1 BCE = -1, 1 CE = +1); ancient-century spans sort from the first bound instead of a midpoint posing as an exact event date. `eventYear` handles decade spans such as `1040s–1090s` correctly. The connected History's merge retains the **first source's display text** in Gold → After Gold → Bitcoin input order, while retaining all source lanes and distinct references.
