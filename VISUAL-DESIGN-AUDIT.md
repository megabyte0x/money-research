# Money Research visual design audit

**Reviewed:** 30 September 2026  
**Site:** https://goldtozcash.vercel.app/  
**Scope:** Live pages at 1280px desktop, 820px tablet, and 390px phone widths; light and dark themes; homepage, volume directory, chapter, comparison, history arc, timeline, takeaways, glossary, sources, mechanics, methods, and search. Findings were checked against the local React and CSS source.  
**Method:** [Taste Skill](https://agenticskills.io/skills/taste-skill), installed locally as `design-taste-frontend` at `~/.codex/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md`. Its redesign protocol calls for an audit before changing the design and for preserving useful brand, content, and accessibility patterns.

## Design direction

Reading this as an **editorial research library for curious readers**, with a restrained, evidence-first visual language. Keep the Newsreader reading voice, IBM Plex Mono for *short* metadata, warm paper palette, sharp edges, and candid evidence status. Aim for more compositional variety and better wayfinding without turning the research into a glossy finance landing page.

Current approximate Taste Skill dials: `DESIGN_VARIANCE 3 / MOTION_INTENSITY 1 / VISUAL_DENSITY 6`. Suggested direction: `5 / 2 / 5`. Motion should explain navigation or state changes; the main lift should come from typography, spacing, page composition, and purposeful diagrams.

## What to preserve

- The quiet serif/mono pairing, paper and ink palette, sharp geometry, and readable long-form measure.
- The chapter summary, source citations, glossary definitions, section links, adjacent-chapter links, and evidence-pending honesty.
- Existing route slugs, navigation labels, research copy, metadata, SEO output, and accessibility features unless a specific change is separately approved.
- The policy of withholding charts whose datasets have not cleared review. Replace their visual absence with sourced explanations or diagrams, never fabricated figures.

## Suggested order of work

1. **Stabilize first paint and navigation.** Align pre-rendered and interactive shells; fix the tablet header and phone comparison flow.
2. **Set design tokens.** Define heading roles, spacing scale, control sizes, rail width, evidence-state treatment, and theme contrast; apply them to all templates.
3. **Recompose entry points.** Improve the homepage, four volume overview cards, and volume directories while keeping article content and routes intact.
4. **Improve research tools.** Redesign the comparison, timeline, mechanics diagrams, and reference list patterns around scanning and comprehension.
5. **Finish the publication shell.** Add a consistent footer/next-step band and verify the complete journey in both themes.

## Acceptance checks for a redesign

- At 390, 820 and 1280px, every main destination is discoverable without hidden, unexplained horizontal navigation; no page-level horizontal overflow.
- On phone, all comparison evidence and limitations can be read without guessing that a table scrolls.
- A cold reload of home, one chapter, comparison, and timeline keeps the same visible title, count, navigation, and approximate layout through hydration.
- The four volumes and a recommended starting point are obvious on the homepage and each directory.
- Long pages have a readable heading hierarchy, useful section navigation, and an intentional ending.
- Light and dark themes preserve legibility for text, controls, focus indicators, evidence status, and diagrams.
- Any new image, figure, or claim has a real source and appropriate scope; missing evidence remains visibly missing.
