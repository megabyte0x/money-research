# Historical-arc chart audit · 14 September 2026

This inventory covers the eleven visual candidates formerly embedded in `src/App.jsx`: ten numeric series/sets and one conceptual figure. They were hidden with `.history-arc figure { display: none }`, but their values and captions still shipped in the app. The dormant renderers, figures and hard-coded values have now been removed. **No historical-arc chart is approved for publication.** Source chapters are reading leads, not datasets.

| Candidate | Former treatment | Audit finding | Rebuild gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Three-metal ladder | Conceptual illustration with bars of unequal width | Universal “gold/silver/copper” roles through 1971 were not established; bar widths looked quantitative without units. | If retained, use a clearly labeled regional/period-specific illustration, with evidence for each role and no magnitude-implying bars. |
| Denarius silver content | Connected line of selected percentages, c. 211 BCE–270 CE | Coin identity, issue-date method, metal-assay sources and uncertainty were not recorded; the “one denarius” line may span changing denominations. | Reconstruct an identified coin series from an assay catalogue, document sample/units/dates and provide a data table; otherwise use separate labeled examples. |
| Gold:silver ratio | One “market ratio” line joining ancient/modern points | Legal mint ratios and market prices were conflated; the 2026 endpoint was a floating “today” value. | Two separately defined series or labeled legal-ratio observations, each with jurisdiction, date, units, method and source. |
| Countries on gold standard | Exact country counts by year | No country roster, inclusion rule or dated dataset supported the precise totals. | Publish a country-by-year roster with definition of convertibility, geographic scope and start/end convention before drawing counts. |
| Bretton Woods gold and dollar claims | Paired approximate bars for 1950, 1960 and August 1971 | Numerators and claims did not have cited archival tables, valuation basis, holder scope or matching observation dates. | Obtain comparable official series and define dollar/gold conversion valuation, foreign-official claim coverage and observation dates. |
| US CPI inflation | Annual percentage line, 1965–85 | Potentially reconstructable, but index, annual-average versus December-to-December method, vintage and exact source were absent. | Recompute from a named BLS/FRED series and convention, version the data, cite the exact table and expose every year in accessible text. |
| Fiat-era crises | Bars of arbitrary heights 1–6 with rescue descriptions | Heights encoded no documented unit or comparable severity measure; monetary, banking and exchange crises were combined. | Replace with a sourced event table, or define one comparable quantitative measure per event before charting. |
| US gross federal debt | Selected line points 1971–2026 | Fiscal-year, calendar-year and intrayear stocks were not distinguished; 2026 was presented without an as-of date. | Rebuild from one Treasury series and observation convention, identify stock versus flow and as-of date, and avoid causal inference from the slope. |
| Reserve composition | Bars for gold, Treasuries, euro and a prior gold point | Mixed dates and potentially unlike denominators; prior-year gold was plotted beside current components. The [ECB chart 7](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/other-publications/ire/html/ecb.ire202606.en.html) comparison in the chapter has an explicit broad-reserves measure and valuation caveat, not this chart's reproducible dataset. | Use the ECB's matching series and periods, state the broad-reserves denominator, keep prior years separate and provide a table. Do not mix with IMF COFER's foreign-exchange-only denominator. |
| Central-bank gold buying | Annual tonnage line 2010–25 | WGC-style numbers were hard-coded without report/table, revision or definition of official-sector net purchases. | Capture a dated primary dataset/version and method, check revisions and cite every reported period. |
| Gold price | Connected selected dollar points 1971–2026, log axis | Fixed parity, intraday peaks, approximate spot prices, a 2025 annual average and 2026 observations were joined as one consistent series. | Use a single dated price convention (e.g. daily close or annual average) and comparable units/currency, or show individually labeled heterogeneous observations without a connecting line. |

To approve any rebuilt chart: store retrievable values with source and exact locator, period, scope, method, units, revision and status; validate definitions and dates; provide a readable data table and accessible alternative; then review the surrounding causal claim separately. Until then, the history page's evidence notice is the public treatment. This audit addresses the unsafe dormant charts, **not** F05's complete dataset/release gate or the plan's separate editorial and accessibility review.

## Release dispositions · 15 September 2026

These are decisions on the **former graphics**, not acceptance of new datasets. The earlier table records the defects and rebuild gates. No former chart has a retrievable, versioned input and transform, an accessible data alternative, a reuse review, and an independent numerical/editorial sign-off together. All eleven therefore stay out of the first release. Two are better replaced by explanatory text or an event table if their content is needed; those replacements are proposals for reader/editorial review, not approved graphics.

| Candidate | Explicit disposition | Reason and evidence needed to change it |
|---|---|---|
| Three-metal ladder | **Sourced conceptual replacement proposed; graphic remains withheld.** | Gold-05 now qualifies metal roles by system and period. A regional, dated prose example could explain denominations without bars; cite each coin/accounting role to an issue or law. Unequal widths must not imply quantities. |
| Denarius silver content | **Remain withheld.** | Gold-06 editor requests issue-specific assays and denomination separation. The former c. 211 BCE–270 CE path cannot be treated as one coin series. Unlock with catalogue issue IDs, assay/sample uncertainty and separately labelled denarius and antoninianus observations. |
| Gold:silver ratio | **Remain withheld.** | Gold-05 removed its mixed numeric ratio table. Legal mint ratios need statute and jurisdiction; a market series needs a common price/date/unit convention. Do not join legal rules to market observations or float a “today” endpoint. |
| Countries on gold standard | **Remain withheld.** | No eligible country-year roster exists. Unlock with jurisdiction roster, gold-convertibility definition, start/end rule, independence/colonial inclusion and source for every transition; only then compute counts. |
| Bretton Woods gold and dollar claims | **Remain withheld.** | The three paired dates lack matched archival valuation and holder/population definitions. A redemption-rate calculation is not itself a stock series. Unlock with comparable gold holdings and foreign-official dollar claims on matching dates and explicit statutory price basis. |
| US CPI inflation | **Reproducible rebuild candidate; former graphic remains withheld.** | [BLS historical CPI-U tables](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/tables/supplemental-files/) identify a possible retrievable input. Choose one dated vintage and U.S.-city-average all-items CPI-U monthly index, calculate annual-average year-on-year change for 1965–85 from all twelve months (including 1964), independently check against BLS published annual averages, and record rounding/base. The existing selected numbers are not that transform. |
| Fiat-era crises | **Sourced event-table replacement proposed; bars remain withheld.** | Arbitrary heights cannot compare crisis severity. A dated event table can distinguish rescue authorization, disbursement and net cost; [GAO's TARP lifetime-cost account](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-24-107033) demonstrates why authorization and net cost differ. Every other event needs its own dated official locator before inclusion. |
| US gross federal debt | **Reproducible rebuild candidate; former graphic remains withheld.** | [Treasury Historical Debt Outstanding](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/datasets/historical-debt-outstanding/) is a fiscal-year-end stock with a pre-1977 July fiscal-year boundary; [Debt to the Penny](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/datasets/debt-to-the-penny/) is a daily stock from 1993. A long line needs a documented single convention and vintage, dates rather than bare years, a 2026 as-of observation, and a check at the overlap. Neither series measures deficits or money creation. |
| Reserve composition | **Remain withheld.** | [ECB June 2026 chart 7](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/other-publications/ire/html/ecb.ire202606.en.html) uses end-2025 broad official reserves and warns that gold and FX datasets differ in country coverage; price valuation materially changes shares. [IMF COFER](https://data.imf.org/Datasets/COFER) excludes monetary gold. Restore only from a matched ECB input/replication with the broad denominator and valuation caveat, not COFER percentages. |
| Central-bank gold buying | **Reproducible rebuild candidate; former graphic remains withheld.** | [WGC FY2025 table 1/central-bank note](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-demand-trends/gold-demand-trends-full-year-2025/central-banks) reports 863.3 tonnes for 2025 and includes estimates of unreported buying, unlike reported monthly reserve changes. [WGC Q2 2026](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-demand-trends/gold-demand-trends-q2-2026/central-banks) revises Q1 from 244t to about 57t. An annual 2010–25 line needs each dated table/vintage, reported-versus-estimated labels and revision checks; one FY2025 total does not validate the old line. |
| Gold price | **Remain withheld.** | [WGC FY2025 table 1](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-demand-trends/gold-demand-trends-full-year-2025) gives a 2025 LBMA annual average, while the old line also used parity and selected peaks. Rebuild only with one price fixing/close convention, frequency, currency, troy-ounce unit and period through a dated endpoint. Gold-09's July/August month-end observations do not establish a continuous daily floor. |

For any future candidate, a text table must expose every plotted observation and its unit/date, followed by a short reading and limitations statement. The caption must identify source, vintage and calculation. Source reuse terms remain unreviewed for these candidates; no data or chart module is shipped. Numerical replication and causal interpretation require separate reviewers. The [package 06 handoff](workstreams/06/HANDOFF.md) records placement and registry requests.
