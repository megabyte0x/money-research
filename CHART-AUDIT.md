# Historical-arc chart audit · 14 September 2026

This inventory covers the eleven visual candidates formerly embedded in `src/App.jsx`: ten numeric series/sets and one conceptual figure. They were hidden with `.history-arc figure { display: none }`, but their values and captions still shipped in the app. The dormant renderers, figures and hard-coded values have now been removed. **No historical-arc chart is approved for publication.** Source chapters are reading leads, not datasets.

| Candidate | Former treatment | Audit finding | Rebuild gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Three-metal ladder | Conceptual illustration with bars of unequal width | Universal “gold/silver/copper” roles through 1971 were not established; bar widths looked quantitative without units. | If retained, use a clearly labeled regional/period-specific illustration, with evidence for each role and no magnitude-implying bars. |
| Denarius silver content | Connected line of selected percentages, c. 211 BCE–270 CE | Coin identity, issue-date method, metal-assay sources and uncertainty were not recorded; the “one denarius” line may span changing denominations. | Reconstruct an identified coin series from an assay catalogue, document sample/units/dates and provide a data table; otherwise use separate labeled examples. |
| Gold:silver ratio | One “market ratio” line joining ancient/modern points | Legal mint ratios and market prices were conflated; the 2026 endpoint was a floating “today” value. | Two separately defined series or labeled legal-ratio observations, each with jurisdiction, date, units, method and source. |
| Countries on gold standard | Exact country counts by year | No country roster, inclusion rule or dated dataset supported the precise totals. | Publish a country-by-year roster with definition of convertibility, geographic scope and start/end convention before drawing counts. |
| Bretton Woods gold and dollar claims | Paired approximate bars for 1950, 1960 and August 1971 | Numerators and claims did not have cited archival tables, valuation basis, holder scope or matching observation dates. | Obtain comparable official series and define dollar/gold conversion valuation, foreign-official claim coverage and observation dates. |
| US CPI inflation | Annual percentage line, 1965–85 | Potentially reconstructable, but index, annual-average versus December-to-December method, vintage and exact source were absent. | Recompute from a named BLS/FRED series and convention, version the data, cite the exact table and expose every year in accessible text. |
| Fiat-era crises | Bars of arbitrary heights 1–6 with rescue descriptions | Heights encoded no documented unit or comparable severity measure; monetary, banking and exchange crises were combined. | Replace with a sourced event table, or define one comparable quantitative measure per event before charting. |
| US gross federal debt | Selected line points 1971–2026 | Fiscal-year, calendar-year and intrayear stocks were not distinguished; 2026 was presented without an as-of date. | Rebuild from one Treasury series and observation convention, identify stock versus flow and as-of date, and avoid causal inference from the slope. |
| Reserve composition | Bars for gold, Treasuries, euro and a prior gold point | Mixed dates and potentially unlike denominators; prior-year gold was plotted beside current components. The [ECB chart 7](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/other-publications/ire/html/ecb.ire202606.en.html) comparison in the chapter has an explicit broad-reserves measure and valuation caveat, not this chart's reproducible dataset. | Use the ECB's matching series and periods, state the broad-reserves denominator, keep prior years separate and provide a table. Do not mix with IMF COFER's foreign-exchange-only denominator. |
| Central-bank gold buying | Annual tonnage line 2010–25 | WGC-style numbers were hard-coded without report/table, revision or definition of official-sector net purchases. | Capture a dated primary dataset/version and method, check revisions and cite every reported period. |
| Gold price | Connected selected dollar points 1971–2026, log axis | Fixed parity, intraday peaks, approximate spot prices, a 2025 annual average and 2026 observations were joined as one consistent series. | Use a single dated price convention (e.g. daily close or annual average) and comparable units/currency, or show individually labeled heterogeneous observations without a connecting line. |

To approve any rebuilt chart: store retrievable values with source and exact locator, period, scope, method, units, revision and status; validate definitions and dates; provide a readable data table and accessible alternative; then review the surrounding causal claim separately. Until then, the history page's evidence notice is the public treatment. This audit addresses the unsafe dormant charts, **not** F05's complete dataset/release gate or the plan's separate editorial and accessibility review.
