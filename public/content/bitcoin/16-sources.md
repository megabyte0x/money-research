## Note on reliability
 
Bitcoin figures move by the minute and every holdings number in these files is a snapshot from the tracker and date cited. Government holdings in particular are contested: China's are probably mostly sold, Ukraine's are officials' private declarations, Bhutan's differ by a factor of ten between trackers, and the US total includes 127,000 coins whose forfeiture is not yet final. Manipulation, wash-trading and lost-coin figures are estimates from the studies named. Statements attributed to officials are quoted or closely paraphrased from the pages cited; a small number of well-known remarks (Villeroy de Galhau, Buffett, Dalio, Paul Tudor Jones) are cited from general record rather than a fetched page. Where a process was unfinished at the time of writing — the CLARITY Act vote, the UK disposal, the Mt. Gox distribution, Strategy's financing, the quantum BIPs, the Texas custody transfer — the files say so.
