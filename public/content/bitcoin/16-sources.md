## Academic and think-tank
 
Warren Weber, *A Bitcoin Standard: Lessons from the Gold Standard*, Bank of Canada Staff Working Paper 2016-14 — https://www.bankofcanada.ca/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/swp2016-14.pdf . The title-page disclaimer makes this the author's staff research, not Bank of Canada policy. Sections 2–4 assume redeemable claims and limited emergency lending; the 2–3% deflation conjecture assumes broad-money growth near Bitcoin issuance, output growth of 2–3% and near-zero velocity growth. The gold-parity example shows fixed weights do not mean a numerically one-to-one exchange rate.

Federal Reserve Board staff, *Taylor Rules* (2007), §2, equations (1)–(2) — https://www.federalreserve.gov/pubs/feds/2007/200718/index.html . Money growth, velocity growth, prices and output enter separately; a fixed base alone is insufficient for a numerical price forecast.

Bank of England, *New forms of digital money* (2021), §1.1 and box A — https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/paper/2021/new-forms-of-digital-money . Deposit creation by banks differs from lending existing funds; fully backed payment instruments could shift credit funding rather than eliminate credit. Applying that distinction to Bitcoin is an inference, not a Bank of England Bitcoin forecast.

## Country cases (files 04, 05, 10)

For government holdings, distinguish custody, beneficial ownership, final forfeiture and placement in reserves. The [6 March 2025 US executive order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/establishment-of-the-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-and-united-states-digital-asset-stockpile/) §3(a) makes only eligible Treasury-held, finally forfeited bitcoin the initial reserve capital; it directs an inventory but does not publish one. The [DOJ's October 2025 Prince Group announcement](https://www.justice.gov/usao-edny/pr/chairman-prince-group-indicted-operating-cambodian-forced-labor-scam-compounds-engaged) reports a civil forfeiture **complaint** for approximately 127,271 bitcoin, not a final judgment. The [UK Crown Prosecution Service](https://www.cps.gov.uk/node/29684) reports approximately 61,000 bitcoin recovered and subject to High Court civil-recovery proceedings; that is not a Treasury reserve. [Druk Holding and Investments](https://www.dhi.bt/newsroom/news/69455944151590a78f28ad59) documents Bhutan's state-linked mining partnership, but not the disposition of every attributed wallet transfer. [Ukraine's NACP](https://nazk.gov.ua/en/news/cash-in-90-currencies-of-the-world-real-estate-abroad-and-gold-reserves-of-officials-nacp-completes-risk-assessment-of-declarations-submitted-for-2025/) describes officials' personal asset declarations, not state-owned bitcoin. No reconciled primary inventory was found here establishing a current PlusToken balance or sale total. Tracker labels and outflows remain leads for investigation, not title records or realised-sale accounts.

## Note on reliability
 
Bitcoin figures move by the minute, and a tracker snapshot is not an audit of legal ownership. Government totals are deliberately withheld where they combine unresolved seizures, finally forfeited assets, attributed state-company wallets, private declarations or different as-of dates. China's PlusToken disposition and Bhutan's realised sales remain unverified here; the US Prince Group coins were subject to a forfeiture complaint, and the UK's recovered coins remain in civil proceedings. Manipulation, wash-trading and lost-coin figures are estimates from the studies named. Statements attributed to officials are quoted or closely paraphrased from the pages cited; a small number of well-known remarks (Villeroy de Galhau, Buffett, Dalio, Paul Tudor Jones) are cited from general record rather than a fetched page. Where a process was unfinished at the time of writing — the CLARITY Act vote, the UK disposal, the Mt. Gox distribution, Strategy's financing, the quantum BIPs, the Texas custody transfer — the files say so.
