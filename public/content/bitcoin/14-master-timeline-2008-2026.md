# 14 — Master Timeline, 2008–2026
 
## Origins and the first structure, 2008–2013
 
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 15 Sep 2008 | Lehman Brothers fails. |
| 31 Oct 2008 | Satoshi Nakamoto posts *Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System*. |
| 3 Jan 2009 | Genesis block mined, embedding The Times headline "Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks." |
| 12 Jan 2009 | First transaction: 10 BTC to Hal Finney (block 170). |
| Oct 2009 | First published exchange rate: 1,309 BTC per dollar, derived from electricity cost. |
| 22 May 2010 | 10,000 BTC paid for two pizzas — first purchase of a physical good. |
| Jul 2010 | Mt. Gox exchange opens in Tokyo. |
| 15 Aug 2010 | Value-overflow bug creates 184 billion BTC in one block; reversed by soft fork within hours. |
| Jun 2011 | Price reaches $31, then falls ~94% to ~$2. |
| Nov 2012 | First halving: subsidy 50 → 25 BTC. |
| Mar 2013 | Chain split (versions 0.7/0.8); the second and last outage in Bitcoin's history. |
| Nov 2013 | Price passes $1,000; Mt. Gox "Willy" bot later found to have bought ~270,000 BTC with non-existent dollars. |
| Dec 2013 | People's Bank of China bars banks from bitcoin. |
 
## Exchanges, forks and the first state responses, 2014–2020
 
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Feb 2014 | Mt. Gox halts withdrawals; ~850,000 BTC lost (200,000 later found); price −36%. |
| 2014 | Tether issues the first dollar stablecoin on Bitcoin's Omni layer; IRS Notice 2014-21 makes bitcoin property for US tax. |
| 2015 | Ethereum launches; price bottoms ~$152 (−87% from 2013 peak). |
| Aug 2016 | Bitfinex hacked; 119,756 BTC stolen; 36% haircut on all customers. |
| Jul 2016 | Second halving: 25 → 12.5 BTC. |
| 2017 | Griffin & Shams later find Tether issuance timed to prop up the price; China bans exchanges and token offerings (Sep). |
| 1 Aug 2017 | Bitcoin Cash hard fork over block size. |
| 24 Aug 2017 | SegWit activates after user-activated soft-fork threat; the "block-size war." |
| 8 Nov 2017 | SegWit2x (miners' and companies' hard fork) cancelled for lack of consensus. |
| Dec 2017 | CME lists bitcoin futures; price peaks at $19,783. |
| 2018 | Price falls 84% to $3,122; Tether trades at $0.88 (Oct); CVE-2018-17144 inflation bug patched before disclosure (Sep). |
| Feb 2019 | QuadrigaCX collapses after founder dies with the only keys; C$216m owed. |
| Mar 2019 | Bitwise tells the SEC 95% of reported bitcoin volume is fake. |
| 2019 | Bhutan's state holding company begins mining with surplus hydropower (disclosed 2023). |
| Mar 2020 | Price falls 50% in two days in the COVID crash, then rises 300% for the year. |
| May 2020 | Third halving: 12.5 → 6.25 BTC. |
| Aug 2020 | MicroStrategy begins converting its treasury into bitcoin. |
| Oct 2020 | Nigeria's EndSARS protest movement switches to bitcoin after bank accounts are frozen. |
 
## Institutions and states arrive, 2021–2023
 
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Feb 2021 | Central Bank of Nigeria bars banks from crypto (reversed Dec 2023); New York AG settles with Tether/Bitfinex ($18.5m). |
| 8–9 Jun 2021 | El Salvador passes the Bitcoin Law (62 of 84 votes). |
| Jun 2021 | China bans mining; hashrate halves; largest difficulty drop on record (Jul). |
| 7 Sep 2021 | Bitcoin becomes legal tender in El Salvador; Chivo wallet with $30 bonus. |
| 24 Sep 2021 | China bans all crypto trading and mining. |
| Oct 2021 | IMF GFSR chapter warns of "cryptoization" in emerging markets; CFTC fines Tether $41m (fully backed 27.6% of days). |
| Nov 2021 | Taproot activates; price peaks at $69,000. |
| Jan 2022 | IMF Article IV tells El Salvador to remove legal-tender status. |
| Feb 2022 | Canada freezes protest donors' accounts and lists 34 crypto wallets; 14.6 BTC already distributed in person. Russia invades Ukraine; Ukraine raises $54m in crypto in a week. |
| 21 Apr 2022 | Central African Republic makes crypto legal tender; CEMAC objects within days. |
| May 2022 | Terra/UST collapses ($40–60bn); Tether trades at $0.945 with $7bn redeemed in five days. |
| 13 Jun 2022 | Celsius freezes withdrawals ($4.7bn liabilities); Three Arrows Capital fails (>$4bn losses); Voyager follows. |
| 8–11 Nov 2022 | FTX halts withdrawals and files for bankruptcy; $8bn customer shortfall. |
| 30 Nov 2022 | ECB blog "Bitcoin's last stand." |
| Dec 2022 | Basel Committee finalises 1,250% risk weight for bitcoin (effective 2025); price bottoms ~$16,500 (−78%); Core Scientific files for bankruptcy. |
| Feb 2023 | IMF "Elements of Effective Policies for Crypto Assets": do not grant legal tender, do not hold in reserves; BIS's Carstens: "the battle has been won." |
| 8–12 Mar 2023 | Silvergate liquidates; USDC depegs to $0.87 on SVB exposure; Signature Bank closed; bitcoin rises 21% in the month. |
| 6 Apr 2023 | CAR repeals crypto legal tender. |
| Aug 2023 | DC Circuit rules for Grayscale against the SEC. |
| Nov 2023 | Binance pleads guilty and pays $4.3bn; Wallet of Satoshi exits the US. |
| Dec 2023 | FASB fair-value accounting standard for crypto (effective 2025); fees spike to $38 on Ordinals demand. |
