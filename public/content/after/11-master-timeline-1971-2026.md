# 11 — Master Timeline: The Fiat Era, 1971–2026

| Date | Event | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| 8 Feb 1971 | The NASD Automated Quotations system (Nasdaq) launches as an electronic market-quotation network. [Nasdaq history](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/nasdaq%3A-50-years-of-market-innovation-2021-02-11) | Electronic market quotes spread to dealers |

| 15 Aug 1971 | Nixon suspends gold convertibility, 10% import surcharge, price freeze | End of Bretton Woods |

| Dec 1971 | Bangladesh independent (taka 1972) | First new state and currency of the era |
| 24 Apr 1972 | European central banks introduce the “snake in the tunnel” intervention mechanism, narrowing participating currencies' exchange-rate bands. | 26 Apr 1973 | Chicago Board Options Exchange opens for listed stock-options trading. [Cboe history](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/the-creation-of-listed-options-at-cboe) | Standardized exchange-traded options |

| 3 May 1973 | SWIFT is incorporated in Brussels as a bank-owned cooperative; its messaging service goes live in 1977. | 31 Dec 1974 | U.S. restrictions on private gold purchase and ownership cease under Public Law 93-373. [Federal Register, 18 Dec 1974, FDIC policy statement](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1974-12-18/pdf/FR-1974-12-18.pdf) | Domestic gold ownership changes separately from bank regulation |
| 1975 | The UK's Retail Prices Index long-run series records 24.2% annual inflation. [ONS series CDSI](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/inflationandpriceindices/timeseries/cdsi) | A UK price-index measure, separate from decolonisation events |
| 25 Jun 1975 | Mozambique becomes independent of Portugal. [UN report](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/861414/files/A_HRC_34_59_Add.2-EN.pdf) | Statehood does not itself date the issue of a new currency |
